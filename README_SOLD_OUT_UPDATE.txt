SOLD OUT UPDATE

The TV and Online Bidding sold-out popup now shows winner name, age, online/offline bid type, Gmail/email, address, and final amount. The supplied SOLD OUT stamp image replaces the hammer. The supplied clapping audio plays at full browser volume for 5 seconds when a new item becomes SOLD OUT. Offline bids can optionally collect age, email, and address so those details can appear on the sold-out screen.
